Why Desi Zaika

The True Essence of Authentic Indian Flavors

❖ ❖ ❖

Situated right next to Raj Garden in Vinayak Nagar, Madar, Desi Zaika is dedicated to reviving royal Mughlai and rustic Rajasthani flavors. Our cooks believe that authentic taste comes from patience, clay-oven heat, and stone-ground spices.

Slow-Cooked Potli & Handi Spices

Our mutton and chicken curries are simmered in clay pots on low embers to infuse every morsel with deep flavor.

Family Dining & Event Catering

Enjoy a comfortable dining environment with family seating or order bulk party packs for celebrations.
